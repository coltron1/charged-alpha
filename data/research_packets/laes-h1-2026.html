Five things to know

  2. The cash-per-share story changes with the denominator. The filed ordinary count plus five-for-one F-share economics and low-strike options gives 231.141M economic shares. Net liquid assets are $2.08 per share before future spending; the quote capitalization divided by price implies only 142.594M outstanding shares. The separate vendor float field is 113.530M shares, not that outstanding count.

The profit bridge

Gross operating expenses were $39.002M before $1.432M of other operating income. That spending supported only $5.408M of gross profit. The issue is not a small rounding gap that modest growth can close. Holding this half-year cost structure and gross margin constant would require roughly $77.5M of half-year sales to break even at the operating line. This is a mechanical stress calculation, not a forecast; management could reduce costs or change the product mix. It shows the scale of operating leverage still required.

Interest income of $6.240M is a benefit of holding a large liquidity pool. It is not proof that the semiconductor operation is near profitability. Currency gains and losses, investment marks and acquisition remeasurement also sit below the operating line. Those items can change without a corresponding shift in product demand. We therefore anchor operating assessment in gross profit, spending and cash flow rather than the smaller net-loss headline.

This route gives no separate value to illiquid investments, preventing double counting their future contribution.

The model deliberately separates cash consumption from capital allocation. The sales route reserves one year of observed FCF burn before assigning operating value; the DCF route instead forecasts future flows directly and does not deduct that burn reserve a second time. The asset route subtracts all liabilities rather than only financial debt. These are different methods with different conservatism, not three copies of the same multiple. A thirty-percent margin of safety against the mean would require about $1.15. That is a price discipline for this uncertainty category, not an automatic buy order.

The bear and bull endpoints are residual-equity stress assumptions and must not be mistaken for mathematically certain floors or ceilings. Cash can be spent, investments may appreciate, and product wins can change the operating value quickly. Conversely, a wide optionality story can justify almost any target if the analyst silently changes both the share count and conversion assumptions. We hold those choices visible and keep the scenario expected value separate from the route mean.

The strongest bull case is not that every opportunity converts. It is that sufficient cash allows SEALSQ to complete qualifications and win a meaningful subset of long-lived security programmes. A higher-margin ASIC and recurring identity mix could improve revenue quality, while acquired capabilities shorten development cycles. In that outcome, today’s expenses fund a durable product base and the strategic portfolio retains or increases its value. Our bull endpoint allows a substantial gain from the snapshot without assuming warrants exercise below their strike.

The strongest bear case is that the company becomes a continuing portfolio of promising projects funded by new equity, with cash burn rising faster than customer demand. Large related-party balances and concentrated voting power make it harder for minority investors to demand a change in strategy. Technical milestones can be real and still fail to produce sufficient economic returns. The stock could therefore lose value even while headline cash and total corporate assets increase.

Where we land is conditional and testable. We see real operating improvement at the gross-profit line, but insufficient evidence that the expanded cost and capital base earns a return for each share. This earns moderate conviction rather than certainty because the commercialization outcome is highly skewed. The SELL call would change if signed commercial conversion, cash collection and per-share capital discipline improve enough to justify revising the model, or if price provides a much larger margin of safety.
