Methods, assumptions & sources

All model assumptions and conventions
  1. Valuation date November 13, 2026 after regular-session close; USD millions and million shares except per-share prices. Price $1.60 at 4:00 p.m. America/New_York (EST), verified by Stock Analysis historical row and Investing.com. It is ex-warrant common; Nasdaq says ex/record November 13 and no exchange-administered price adjustment. No warrant value is subtracted again and this buyer receives no separate warrant distribution.
  2. Operating forecast periods are calendar approximations of issuer fiscal years (differences up to several days), not quoted fiscal guidance. 2027 starts at $75m/$85m base/bull, within management FY2027 $65–85m guide; bear $60m misses it. Revenue is modeled evenly within each calendar year; actual shipments are lumpy. Q4 stub uses the $9.5m midpoint over 91 fiscal-quarter days through January 3, 2027.
  3. We do not pretend the company has disclosed segment revenue, ASPs, accepted-unit capacity or forecast margins. Korean, eyewear and phone revenue allocations are analyst assumptions. The base requires Korea $72m→$465m and eyewear $3m→$345m in 2027–2036; phones contribute zero. This is still rapid growth, not a flat-current-loss model. The bull requires phone requalification and $1.45bn phone sales by 2036, plus $700m Korea/$650m eyewear; this is an explicitly conditional upside, not an order book.
  4. Product gross margins in the forecast are economic margins before stock compensation allocated centrally to operating costs, not forecasts of reported GAAP gross margin. Base Korea matures at 35%, eyewear 42%; bull Korea 38%, eyewear 45%, phones 35%. Historical GAAP and adjusted margins remain labeled separately.
  5. Base economic operating expense falls $140m in 2027 to $110m in 2029, then rises to $165m by 2036. This assumes substantial additional portfolio prioritization and cost reduction if phones do not requalify. It is more than the disclosed $8.5m gross annual restructuring saving and is NOT management guidance. It gives management credit for adapting, rather than retaining phone-development costs forever without phone revenue. Bull expenses grow $142m→$470m to support the successful platform.
  6. Economic operating costs exclude amortization of already-granted awards, whose claim is recognized in starting shares. They include a separately assumed cost for NEW employee grants/replacement compensation: $12m, $16m, $25m in 2027–2029; thereafter 4% of revenue (base minimum $25m; bull minimum $30m), included within total operating expenses, not added again.
