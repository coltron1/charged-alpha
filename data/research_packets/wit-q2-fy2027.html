We do not know the issuer’s exact publication clock or release session, so that return is described as a calendar-day observation rather than a clean post-release reaction. The quote endpoint’s percentage differed from the historical close series. We use one consistent daily series for the chart and recompute the changes instead of choosing whichever percentage makes the story more dramatic.

The share class also matters. Each NYSE ADS represents one Wipro equity share, according to the annual report. The Indian share price, rupee earnings and dollar ADS price must be connected through an explicit exchange rate. A low dollar share price says nothing by itself about business cheapness. Our valuation uses ₹95.83 per dollar, the current statements’ convenience rate, and the actual period-end share count. Historical prices are retained as delivered by the provider; they are not mixed with unadjusted old per-share earnings.

The print

Per-share improvement and company-wide profit point in different directions.

Revenue reached ₹247.5 billion, up 9.1% from the year-earlier quarter. Total profit was ₹32.0 billion, down 1.9%, while basic EPS increased from ₹3.10 to ₹3.23. Those measures are not inconsistent. EPS belongs to common shareholders and uses a weighted share denominator; the headline profit includes noncontrolling interests. Parent-attributable profit declined 1.4%, while weighted basic shares fell 5.5%. The denominator more than offset the weaker numerator.

Holding the old weighted share count constant, current parent profit would produce roughly ₹3.06 per share. That calculation is not an alternative accounting EPS figure. It isolates the mechanical share-count contribution so readers can judge the operating result separately from capital allocation. The June buyback was completed before this quarter and therefore influences the full-quarter denominator more than it did the preceding quarter.

The company’s economically important IT Services revenue was $2,611.5 million, nearly unchanged sequentially in dollars and flat in constant currency. The next-quarter constant-currency range is −1.5% to +0.5%. Its midpoint is below zero, which does not support calling this a clear demand recovery. Meanwhile, provider earnings estimates use dollar conversions that do not precisely reconcile to the issuer’s presentation. We retain them as context, but do not manufacture a clean revenue beat or EPS miss by comparing incompatible currency bases.

Segments and demand

Compare the current structure with restated prior periods.

Americas 1 and Americas 2 together remain a large part of the services business, but their definitions changed at the start of this fiscal year. Wipro aligned Latin American and Canadian customers with the relevant Americas industry groups and moved certain sectors within that structure. The latest issuer tables restate the comparable periods. We use those restated numbers for current regional comparisons, rather than interpret a classification transfer as organic growth.

Constant-currency year-over-year growth was negative in both Americas units and positive in Europe and APMEA. Europe’s segment profit improved much more than its revenue, which helps the consolidated result. That is encouraging, but it also makes the durability of regional cost and contract economics important. The regional segment revenue table includes operating foreign-exchange gains; the reconciliation removes those gains to reach consolidated reported revenue. Comparing the segment total directly with reported sales without that adjustment would double count an accounting presentation difference.

Bookings are the harder forward-looking issue. Total contract value was $2.595 billion and large-deal contract value was $674 million. Total bookings divided by quarterly services revenue is about 0.99 times. This is a directional demand indicator, not a backlog coverage ratio: bookings include renewals, changes and contracts with different lives, and do not subtract later cancellations from old bookings. A weak quarter can be timing, but it should be followed by conversion evidence before investors extrapolate the earlier large awards.

The profit bridge

Track the money from operations to each share.

Operating profit was ₹40.645 billion. Finance expenses absorbed ₹5.454 billion, finance and other income added ₹6.770 billion, and equity-method income added ₹86 million. Tax then reduced pre-tax profit to ₹31.997 billion. A small noncontrolling loss makes parent-attributable profit slightly higher than total profit. Every step matters because an operating-margin improvement can coexist with a weaker bottom line when financing income and costs move the other way.

The quarter’s finance expense increased from the prior year, while finance and other income declined. A large capital return can reduce the pool of cash and investments earning income, even when it improves per-share arithmetic. That does not make the buyback automatically bad; it means its economics should be judged against the price paid and the return on retained liquidity. The completed offer paid ₹250 per share. Our current valuation is expressed in dollars at a later exchange rate, so comparing those amounts requires explicit conversion and a different date assumption.

The operating margin most often quoted by management belongs to IT Services. Consolidated operating margin is a related but different measure because products and reconciling items remain. We do not label the services measure as adjusted consolidated profit. Nor do we add back stock compensation simply because it is noncash in the current period. The statement’s ordinary expenses remain in the earnings routes, and the owner-cash route makes a separate conservative dilution charge.

Earnings quality

Strong cash collection does not remove demand or allocation risk.

Quarterly operating cash flow was ₹53.048 billion, or 165.8% of total profit. It is a strong collection result and deserves weight in the analysis. It should not be projected unchanged into every future quarter. Customer billing, collections, taxes and working-capital movements are uneven, and the company’s annual cash-flow record shows why a rolling period is useful. The owner-cash model therefore uses the trailing year rather than annualizing this unusually strong quarter.

Receivable days below use ending trade receivables divided by quarterly consolidated revenue and multiplied by a standardized quarter of 91 days. That is an analytical proxy, not management’s contractual collection metric. Adding current unbilled balances and contract assets produces a much larger exposure to work not yet converted into cash. We show both concepts rather than imply that billed receivables capture the entire funding requirement. Acquisition effects and currency translation can change the balance sheet without flowing through cash movements in the same way.

The quality scorecard deliberately separates cash realization from growth quality. Low stock compensation and a stable tax rate can be favorable even when the demand outlook is weak. A small minority adjustment is not a material profit leak this quarter. The main questions are whether cash conversion persists, whether new work replaces recognized revenue and whether capital distributions remain sensible after considering acquisitions and liquidity. There is no need to invent a non-GAAP reconciliation when the current release does not use that bridge for its headline net income.

Cash and capital allocation

Count the obligations before calling liquidity excess cash.

Wipro ended September with ₹95.016 billion of cash and ₹341.527 billion of current investments. Our liquidity bridge subtracts ₹185.021 billion of borrowings and ₹36.044 billion of lease liabilities. It leaves ₹215.478 billion, or approximately $2.25 billion and $0.227 per ADS at the stated exchange rate. We exclude noncurrent investments, associates and goodwill from this conservative liquid-resource measure. They may have value, but they are not interchangeable with cash available for distribution tomorrow.

First-half operating cash less gross capital spending was ₹78.288 billion. Buyback cash and parent dividends together exceeded that amount by more than two times. The business can fund an unusually large return from accumulated investments; the observation is not a solvency alarm. It does show why the buyback cannot be extrapolated as a recurring yield financed solely by current-period free cash flow. The stock’s lower share count comes with fewer liquid assets available to earn income or finance the next acquisition.

Acquisitions remain part of the operating story. Mindsprint and selected Alpha Net customer contracts closed during the first half, while Harman’s DTS acquisition closed in the preceding fiscal year. They bring capabilities and client relationships but also integration work, goodwill and amortization. Ordinary acquisitions do not invalidate analysis of the continuing parent. They do require us to distinguish organic demand from purchased exposure and to avoid treating all amortization as economically irrelevant. Goodwill and intangibles together equal a substantial portion of book equity, so book value is not a liquidation floor.

Valuation

Three explicit routes; no cash double count.

Our $1.85 base fair value is a judgment informed by three routes, not a price target borrowed from an old brokerage note. The operating-profit route values trailing consolidated EBIT at 10 times and adds our conservative net liquid cash. The earnings route applies 14 times to an authored normalized annual EPS assumption of ₹13.20. The owner-cash route discounts cash already after financing costs, leases and a conservative stock-compensation deduction. Because that last route is an equity cash-flow model, it receives no additional net-cash bonus.

The multiples and discount rate are our assumptions. They reflect a business that generates cash and has a large client base, but has not yet demonstrated sustained growth. We do not capitalize the latest quarterly cash-conversion ratio as if it were permanent. Nor do we assume the buyback repeats every year. The trailing-year base dampens the seasonal collections effect while still recognizing the improved recent cash result. A stronger growth trajectory could support a higher multiple; a persistent decline could justify lower earnings and a lower multiple at the same time.

At $1.87, subtracting the liquid-resource bridge leaves an enterprise value equivalent to about 9.9 times trailing operating profit. At a 10-times multiple, the price requires a consolidated margin near 16.1% on trailing revenue. That is not an extravagant hurdle, but it also means the market already pays for reasonable stability. Our high uncertainty rating calls for a meaningful cushion before a price-only upgrade. A 20% discount to the base is $1.48, contingent on the underlying earnings and liquidity thesis remaining intact.

Management scorecard

Capital allocation and operating delivery need separate grades.

Management delivered services revenue within the prior range after the issuer’s currency restatement. That deserves credit for near-term forecasting, but it is not the same as establishing a positive demand trend. The new growth range remains cautious. The practical test is whether recent awards turn into revenue without a deterioration in contract profitability or an unusual working-capital investment.

The services margin improved sequentially while headcount declined. A smaller workforce can reflect productivity, integration, attrition or demand conditions; the published headcount alone does not identify the cause. We therefore avoid claiming an AI productivity dividend solely from a staffing reduction. Utilization and attrition provide useful context, but the relevant proof remains sustainable client revenue, operating economics and cash collection. Claims about AI capabilities belong in the strategy discussion until the financial statements show their incremental return.

Ownership concentration is substantial. The annual report attributes 72.62% beneficial ownership to Azim H. Premji, including related holdings, as of March 31. That figure is not a fresh post-buyback ownership calculation. It informs governance and capital-allocation risk without assuming that all related entities vote identically on every issue. The annual report identifies Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP as auditor and states the ADS relationship clearly. Ordinary shareholders still face currency, tax, governance and depositary considerations alongside business performance.

Ranked risks

The strongest bull case still needs conversion evidence.

The strongest positive case is that Wipro has a cash-generating delivery platform, repeat customer relationships and enough financial resources to invest while demand is uneven. Strong collections and sequential margin improvement are genuine evidence in that case. If bookings normalize and the current workforce supports higher utilization without sacrificing quality, profit can grow faster than a modest revenue recovery. The bull valuation allows both better margins and a higher multiple for sustained improvement.

The strongest negative case is that weak bookings foreshadow another period of stagnant services revenue while pricing, wage costs and AI-driven delivery changes pressure economics. Buybacks can support EPS temporarily, but they cannot replace profitable client demand indefinitely. Acquired relationships may require more investment than expected, and goodwill does not create cash on its own. In that case, both normalized earnings and the multiple deserve scrutiny, not just a small adjustment to the share count.

Our base case sits between those outcomes. We are not forecasting a liquidity crisis, and we are not paying in advance for a strong growth cycle. The most useful disconfirming evidence would be a sustained break in cash conversion, a material drop in margins or fresh bookings consistently below revenue. Conversely, a repeatable return to positive constant-currency growth with bookings replacement and disciplined capital spending would weaken the cautious case. Each conclusion remains conditional on the same share class, currency basis and current facts.

Appendix and method

Traceable units, source limits and reproducible calculations.

Financial tables use millions of Indian rupees unless a heading explicitly says dollars, billions or per share. The company’s dollar convenience translations are not a second set of economic earnings. IT Services revenue and bookings are separately reported dollar operating measures. EPS comparisons use the issuer’s later bonus-restated figures where required; original pre-bonus values remain in the research dataset so the adjustment can be audited.

Quarterly cash flow is calculated from cumulative statements by subtracting the preceding cumulative period. Free cash flow before leases means operating cash less gross capital expenditure. Owner cash in the valuation also subtracts lease payments and stock compensation. These are analytical definitions, not additional IFRS subtotals. Ending-balance receivable days are a standardized proxy and should not be confused with a management collection statistic.

The current segment structure is compared with the issuer’s restated prior-year data. Older regional mixes remain in the dataset as originally reported and are not used to imply like-for-like growth across the classification change. The annual report supplies the ADS ratio, governance and audited cash-flow history. Current quarterly statements supply the latest share count, debt, leases and acquisition information. Provider estimates, targets and ratings retain their separate dates and limitations.

The valuation is reproducible from the saved dataset, but reproducibility does not make assumptions certain. Multiples, discount rates, growth, scenario probabilities and decision thresholds are authored judgments. We publish the sensitivity and reverse case so a reader can substitute a different view without hiding the arithmetic. No price target is guaranteed, and no quarterly scorecard substitutes for an investor’s own risk, time horizon and diversification decisions.
