Charged Alpha Privacy Policy

Effective July 31, 2026

Charged Alpha does not ask for brokerage credentials and does not execute trades. Your course progress is stored primarily on your device. The only personal information we collect is an email address - and only if you choose to give it to us for updates.

This policy is published by Crown Creek Capital LLC, doing business as Charged Alpha ("we," "us," "our"), and covers both the Charged Alpha mobile app (the "App") and the chargedalpha.com website (the "Site").

Information used by the app

Learning progress and settings: completed pages, scores, badges, playback state, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Purchase status: the App uses RevenueCat to determine whether Premium is active and to restore purchases. RevenueCat may process a pseudonymous app-user identifier, product and transaction information, entitlement status, platform, app version, and limited device metadata.

Store transactions: Apple or Google processes payment details. Charged Alpha does not receive your full card or bank-account number.

Requested market information: where live-data tools are available, a ticker or company query and the related data request may be sent to a market-data provider. Do not enter personal information into a ticker search.

Email updates (optional): if you choose to subscribe - from the welcome screen or the Settings toggle - the email address you enter is sent to Charged Alpha's backend and stored so we can send product news, new lessons, and investing research. Providing an email is never required to use the App, and you can opt out at any time in Settings or from any email we send.

Information used by the website

The chargedalpha.com website is separate from the offline app. The Site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic, page performance, and how visitors use public pages. The Site may also use basic cookies or similar browser storage for security, sessions, preferences, and normal functionality. Public pages can include embedded YouTube videos or subscribe buttons; when those load or are used, YouTube/Google may receive information according to Google's own privacy practices.

How information is used

Information is used to preserve course progress, provide requested narration and interactions, validate Premium access, restore purchases, deliver the email updates you request, understand aggregate website traffic, troubleshoot reliability, and improve our products. Charged Alpha does not sell personal information and does not use course activity for third-party targeted advertising.

Service providers

Purchase and entitlement processing may involve RevenueCat, Apple, or Google. If you opt in to email updates, your address is processed by our hosting infrastructure and may be processed by an email-delivery provider solely to deliver those messages. The website uses Google Analytics and may load embedded YouTube content. These providers process information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear it in the App, remove the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to device backups. Store transaction records and entitlement history may be retained by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat for accounting, fraud prevention, restoration, and legal obligations. Email subscriptions are retained while active; after an opt-out, a limited suppression record may be retained so we continue to honor that choice. To request access to or deletion of data associated with the App, use the App Support link on the Charged Alpha store listing or contact us below.

Children and international use

Charged Alpha is a general-audience financial education product and is not directed to children under 13. If local law provides additional privacy rights, you may exercise them through the contact below.

Security and changes

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Material policy changes will be reflected by an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Crown Creek Capital LLC d/b/a Charged Alpha at hello@chargedalpha.com.

Charged Alpha is a brand of Crown Creek Capital LLC, a Minnesota limited liability company. Governing law: State of Minnesota, USA.
