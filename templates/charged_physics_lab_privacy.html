Charged Physics Lab

Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Charged Physics Lab does not require an account, does not include advertising or analytics, and does not send your formula work or learning progress to us.

This policy is published by Crown Creek Capital LLC, doing business as Charged Alpha ("we," "us," "our"), and covers the Charged Physics Lab mobile app (the "App"). The separate chargedalpha.com website is covered by the Charged Alpha website and app privacy policy.

Information kept on your device

The App stores formula cards, entered values, results, linked formula steps, visited-formula progress, narration resume state, and preferences such as subtitles, tilt, haptics, and sound in platform-managed local app storage. A bounded local reliability log records app lifecycle, navigation, narration, and error events so failures can be diagnosed on that device. The current App contains no mechanism that uploads this information to us or a third party.

Device features

If you allow motion access, the App reads device motion or orientation to create a visual tilt effect. Those readings are processed transiently on the device and are not saved or transmitted. Denying motion access does not prevent formula search, calculations, diagrams, or narration. The App also uses device audio output and may use haptic feedback for interface cues.

Network use and service providers

Core formulas, worked examples, ElevenLabs-generated narration, sound effects, and fonts are packaged with the App. The current production build has no account, advertising, analytics, social-login, speech-service, or application-service endpoint. Apple and Google may process store downloads, operating-system diagnostics, and backups under their own policies; we do not receive your formula work from those platform services.

Backups, retention, and deletion

Depending on your device and platform-account settings, local app data may be included in an operating-system backup or device-to-device transfer. We do not receive or control that backup. Local data remains until it is replaced, cleared through device controls, or removed when the App is uninstalled, subject to platform backup retention. Because the App has no account or server-side learner profile, there is no remote profile for us to delete.

Collection, sharing, and sale

Based on the current production App, we do not collect, share, sell, or use personal data for tracking or targeted advertising.

Children's privacy

Charged Physics Lab is a general educational tool intended for learners age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Security and changes

The App limits its learning state to platform-managed local storage, but no storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. If the App's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated before that version is released.
