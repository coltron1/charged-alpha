Why Charged Alpha exists

I wanted better earnings analysis I could actually keep up with.

I use frontier AI analysis to create standardized video presentations reviewing more than 1,000 stocks every quarter. The goal is to make earnings reports easier to follow, compare, and revisit without requiring people to spend their whole day reading filings and transcripts.

As I dug deeper into the tools of AI and coding, I started building out the rest of the Charged Alpha platform around investing analysis and education.

That eventually led to the free investing games: interactive market-history challenges that teach investing principles and concepts in a more engaging way than a static lesson.

How I think about education

Good market lessons should slow down the obvious answer.

  • Start with evidence and historical context.
  • Show the trade-off, not just the outcome.
  • Make risk, timing, and position sizing visible.
  • Keep educational resources useful without a paid gate.
  • All Charged Alpha content is educational only and is not financial advice.
